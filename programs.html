What we do, in detail

Four activities, all carried out for the company's own account.

Program 01 · Held, and governed

Business interests

The company holds interests in operating businesses and maintains the corporate side properly: registrations current, decisions documented, structures reviewed as things change. It's unglamorous work that becomes expensive when neglected.

  • Own account
  • Internal function
  • Privately held

Program 02 · Premises and assets

Property interests

Where a business operates from premises, owning them removes a landlord from the equation and keeps the cost predictable. Property is maintained on a planned cycle using licensed trades, and anything leased to third parties is let on written terms in line with fair housing law.

  • Ongoing
  • Own account
  • Not a service
  • Licensed trades engaged

Program 03 · One back office

Support to the businesses

Small operating companies rarely justify their own finance or admin function. Running it once for the group means renewals get tracked, months get closed on a schedule, and owners see a consolidated picture rather than five separate impressions.

  • Group companies
  • Internal function
  • Not sold externally
