How we work

Privately held, and plain about it

Zebib Holdings LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations. It is privately held and acts for its own account.

It is not a fund, a registered investment adviser, a broker-dealer or a licensed real estate brokerage. It manages no money for anyone else, offers no security to anyone, and provides no investment, legal or tax advice. Those services come from licensed professionals the group engages.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can I invest in the group?

No. The company is privately held and makes no offer of any security or investment to anyone.

Can I buy services from you?

No. The administrative and planning work described here is provided to companies within the group only.

What does the group own?

Business and property interests held for the long term. We'll describe them in a private conversation where there's a reason to.

Are you a real estate brokerage?

No. Property is bought and held for our own account. We represent nobody and charge no commission.

Do you give investment advice?

No, to anyone. Nothing on this site is advice of any kind.

How do opportunities reach you?

Through operators, advisers and professionals the group already works with, privately and case by case.

Something relevant to discuss?

A private conversation, with a clear answer either way and no obligation.
